# Authorship and source handling

This repository contains an independent study collection maintained by the repository owner (`fobos123deimos`). The course association is supplied by the owner. The explanations, exercises, numerical experiments and diagrams were prepared for this collection.

The supplied integrated syllabus is itself a non-official study synthesis. It provides the topic sequence and bibliography, not evidence of an official teaching plan. Professor Michela Mulas is acknowledged for course context only; authorship, review and endorsement of the new collection are not attributed to her.

Standard methods and the physical benchmark models are attributed to their scientific references, not presented as new discoveries. The Kroll–Schulte paper and received course PDF are kept outside this repository.

Third-party rotor data retain their license and provenance in `benchmarks/data`. Other external packages are acquired through documented source interfaces and remain subject to their own terms. No repository-wide license is assigned to those third-party materials.
