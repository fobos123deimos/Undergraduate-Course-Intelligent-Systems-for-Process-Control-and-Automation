# References and guided viewing

The twelve books below reproduce the bibliography supplied with the integrated study syllabus. Edition/year labels follow that source and should be checked against the particular library copy. The benchmark article is the primary source for the separate benchmark track.

## Core bibliography

1. Massa de Campos, M.; Saito, K. *Sistemas inteligentes em controle e automação de processos*. Ciência Moderna, 2004.
2. Nascimento Jr., C. L.; Yoneyama, T. *Inteligência artificial em controle e automação*. Blücher, 2000.
3. Nørgaard, M.; Ravn, O.; Poulsen, N. K.; Hansen, L. K. *Neural Networks for Modelling and Control of Dynamic Systems*. Springer, 2009 (year recorded in the supplied syllabus).
4. Passino, K.; Yurkovich, S. *Fuzzy Control*. Pearson, 1997 (year recorded in the supplied syllabus).
5. Vidal, L. C. *Aplicação Fuzzy x PID: Técnicas de Controle em Ambientes Simulados*. Appris, 2015. ISBN 978-8581927800.

## Benchmark source

Kroll, A.; Schulte, H. *Benchmark problems for nonlinear system identification and control using Soft Computing methods: Need and overview*. Applied Soft Computing 25, 496–513, 2014. [DOI and publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asoc.2014.08.034). Its metric named MAE denotes **maximum** absolute error; this repository explicitly separates `max_abs_error` from `mean_abs_error` to avoid the common ambiguity.
